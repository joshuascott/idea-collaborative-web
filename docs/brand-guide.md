# 1. Brand Foundation

## What we are

Idea Collaborative helps small and mid-sized businesses in Northern Colorado put AI to work in weeks instead of years. Enterprise-grade expertise, delivered at SMB scale, by someone who lives here.

## Positioning statement

> For owners and operators of 5 to 100 person businesses in Loveland, Fort Collins, and Windsor who know AI matters but do not know where to start, Idea Collaborative is a local AI consultancy that turns AI from a headline into a working process. Unlike national training vendors and generalist IT shops, we bring 25+ years of enterprise solutions experience and stay accountable for results after the workshop ends.

## Brand promise

**Practical AI that pays for itself.**

This is the tagline and the standard. If a recommendation cannot be tied to hours saved, revenue gained, or errors reduced, it does not go in the deck.

## Four pillars

| Pillar | What it means | How it shows up |
|---|---|---|
| **Practical** | Working tools, not theory. Every session ends with something the client can use Monday. | Live demos, take-home prompt libraries, 30-day action plans |
| **Local** | A neighbor, not a vendor. Northern Colorado examples, in-person delivery, community partnerships. | Named towns, local business scenarios, chamber and CSU partnerships |
| **Proven** | 25+ years and 25+ industries behind every recommendation. | Enterprise credentials stated plainly, case studies with numbers |
| **Honest** | Integrity and stewardship over the quick sale. We will tell a client when AI is not the answer. | No hype language, no invented urgency, transparent pricing |

## Audience

- **Primary:** Owners, department heads, and operations managers at businesses with 5 to 100 employees
- **Secondary:** Realtors and small professional services firms as an entry vertical
- **Mindset:** Curious but skeptical, time-poor, burned by past tech promises, unsure what is real
- **What they fear:** Wasting money, looking foolish, being left behind, having to become technical

## Personality

Idea Collaborative is the experienced operator at the table, not the AI evangelist on stage. Confident without swagger. Plain-spoken. Generous with what it knows. Comfortable saying "that will not work for you."

**We are:** clear, grounded, useful, warm, credible
**We are not:** hyped, jargon-heavy, doom-laden, salesy, cute

# 3. Logo

## The mark

An interlocking loop formed from two arcs: a deep navy arc and a teal arc that resolve into a speech-bubble tail at lower right. It reads as continuity and conversation at the same time, which is the business in one glyph: an ongoing collaborative relationship, not a one-time transaction. Do not explain it in marketing copy. Let it work.

## Files on hand

| File | Format | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `logo_transparent.svg` | Vector, transparent | Print, large format, web. This is the master. Scale from this file |
| `ic_logo_transparent.png` | Raster 1024px, transparent | Slides, social avatars, email signatures, anywhere vector is not accepted |

**Still needed:** a horizontal lockup (mark + wordmark side by side), a reversed one-color white version for photography and dark gradients, and a favicon-safe 32px crop. Commission these before the first paid workshop.

## Lockups

1. **Mark alone.** Social avatars, favicons, small badges, the top-left corner of a flyer panel.
2. **Stacked lockup.** Mark above **idea**collab, centered, with the tagline beneath. Use where vertical space allows.
3. **Horizontal lockup.** Mark to the left of **idea**collab, optically centered on the wordmark's x-height. Preferred for slide headers, letterhead, and web navigation.

## Clear space

Reserve clear space on all sides equal to the height of the inner counter (the open circle inside either loop). Nothing enters that zone: no text, no rule lines, no QR codes, no photo edges.

## Minimum sizes

| Context | Minimum |
|---|---|
| Mark alone, digital | 32 px wide |
| Mark alone, print | 0.375 in wide |
| Horizontal lockup, digital | 140 px wide |
| Horizontal lockup, print | 1.25 in wide |

## Logo don'ts

- Do not recolor the mark outside the approved palette. Navy and teal, or a single flat white on dark, or a single navy on light. Nothing else.
- Do not rotate, skew, stretch, or add perspective.
- Do not add drop shadows, glows, bevels, or outlines.
- Do not place the two-color mark on a mid-teal background where the teal arc disappears. Use the white one-color version.
- Do not enclose the mark in a box, circle, or badge unless it is a required avatar crop.
- Do not typeset the wordmark in a font other than the approved brand typeface.

# 4. Color

## Core palette

| Name | Hex | RGB | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Deep Navy** | `#1C3E54` | 28, 62, 84 | Primary. Headlines, body text on light, dark panel backgrounds, the navy arc |
| **Signal Teal** | `#3C8E8E` | 60, 142, 142 | Secondary. Buttons, accents, the teal arc, gradient endpoint |
| **Deep Teal** | `#2C6671` | 44, 102, 113 | Bridge tone. Where navy and teal overlap in the mark. Use for teal-colored *text* on light backgrounds and for button hover states |

## Extended palette

| Name | Hex | Role | Contrast notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#0F2836` | Deepest surface, footer bars | 15.25:1 on white |
| Teal Light | `#7FC5C5` | Accent text and rules on navy | 5.73:1 on navy. Passes AA |
| Teal Pale | `#A8DADA` | Secondary text on navy, decorative fills | 7.34:1 on navy |
| Mist | `#E6F2F2` | Tinted card backgrounds on white | Background only |
| Cloud | `#F4F7F8` | Page background, alternating sections | Background only |
| Slate | `#4F5D68` | Body copy, captions, form labels on light | 6.78:1 on white |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Reversed text, light panel background | 11.25:1 on navy |

## Signature gradient

The flyer's left panel is the brand's most recognizable surface. Specify it precisely so it reproduces the same way every time.

```
linear-gradient(135deg, #1C3E54 0%, #2C6671 55%, #3C8E8E 100%)
```

Navy anchors the top-left where the logo and headline sit. Teal opens toward the bottom-right. Use it for flyer panels, slide title backgrounds, web hero sections, and email headers. Never run body copy longer than a short paragraph across the teal end of the gradient; move it to a white panel or a navy-anchored area.

## Accessibility rules

These are not suggestions. Half the audience is over 45 and reading a flyer in a chamber lobby.

- **Signal Teal `#3C8E8E` fails AA against white at 3.85:1.** Do not set body text in it on a white background. Use it for large display type at 24px+ and for solid button fills with white text only when the button label is 18px bold or larger. For teal-toned text on white, use Deep Teal `#2C6671` at 6.47:1.
- **Never set navy text on teal or teal text on navy.** That pair is 2.92:1 and unreadable.
- Reversed white text on Deep Navy is the strongest combination available. Default to it for dark panels.
- Every printed piece must survive grayscale. Test the flyer as a black-and-white photocopy before ordering a run.

## Color proportion

Roughly 60% neutral (white, Cloud, Mist), 30% Deep Navy, 10% Signal Teal. Teal is punctuation. When everything is accented, nothing is.

# 5. Typography

The flyer PDF ships without embedded fonts, which means it will re-flow differently on other machines. Lock the typeface now.

## Recommended: Inter

Free, open-licensed, available in Google Fonts, Figma, Canva, and Google Workspace. Its geometry matches the flyer's current look closely enough that nothing already printed will feel orphaned. Available as a web font, a desktop install, and inside Notion exports.

**Alternate for headline warmth:** Poppins for the wordmark and display headlines only, paired with Inter for everything else. Choose one path and hold it.

## Type scale

| Level | Size / Line height | Weight | Color | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Display | 40 / 46 | 700 | White on navy, Navy on light | Flyer headline, slide title |
| H1 | 32 / 40 | 700 | Deep Navy | Page and document titles |
| H2 | 24 / 32 | 600 | Deep Navy | Section heads |
| H3 | 19 / 28 | 600 | Deep Teal | Sub-sections |
| Body | 16 / 26 | 400 | Slate or Deep Navy | Paragraphs |
| Small | 14 / 22 | 400 | Slate | Captions, disclaimers, contact blocks |
| Label | 12 / 16, +0.08em tracking | 600 uppercase | Deep Teal | Eyebrows, tier labels, category tags |

## The wordmark

**idea** in weight 700, **collab** in weight 300 or 400, set as one continuous word with no space. On dark backgrounds, "idea" is White and "collab" is Teal Light `#7FC5C5`. On light backgrounds, "idea" is Deep Navy and "collab" is Deep Teal `#2C6671`. Tighten tracking slightly, around -0.02em. Always lowercase. Never all-caps, never title case.

## Typographic rules

- Left-align body copy. Never justify. Never center a paragraph longer than two lines.
- Body copy lines run 55 to 75 characters. The flyer currently runs long and wraps awkwardly on "Years."
- One display headline per surface.
- Sentence case for headlines, not Title Case. "Transform your business with AI in weeks, not years" reads more like a person talking.
- Checkmark bullets (✓) in Signal Teal are an established brand device on the flyer. Keep them for benefit lists only. Use standard bullets for everything else.

# 6. Layout System

## The split panel

The half-page flyer establishes the core layout: a gradient panel carrying identity and message on the left, a white panel carrying proof and action on the right. Roughly a 2:1 ratio. This translates directly to slide masters, web heroes, and one-page case studies.

**Left (gradient):** logo, wordmark, tagline, headline, benefit bullets, tier cards
**Right (white):** QR code, URL, primary CTA button, contact block

## Tier cards

Three equal-width cards in a row, semi-transparent white over the gradient, no border radius beyond 6px. Tier name in 600 weight, price or "Call for More Info" beneath in a lighter weight at reduced opacity. Order is always Level 1, Level 2, Level 3, left to right, cheapest to most expensive.

## Buttons

- **Primary:** Signal Teal fill, white label, 600 weight, 8px radius, generous horizontal padding. Hover to Deep Teal `#2C6671`.
- **Secondary on dark:** transparent fill, 1.5px Teal Light border, white label.
- **Secondary on light:** transparent fill, 1.5px Deep Navy border, Deep Navy label.

Button labels are verbs. "Book Your Workshop." "Get the Readiness Assessment." "Reserve a Seat." Never "Submit," "Click Here," or "Learn More" on a primary action.

## QR codes

Black modules on white, minimum 1 inch square in print, with a short caption above ("Scan to Learn More") and the plain URL beneath so people who dislike QR codes still have a path. Never place a QR code on the gradient.

## Whitespace

The current flyer has a large empty region below the tier cards. Either let that breathe deliberately or fill it with one specific proof element: a named testimonial, a single metric, or a next workshop date. Do not fill it with more bullets.

# 7. Voice

## Voice attributes

**Clear.** Short sentences. Concrete nouns. If a sentence needs a second read, rewrite it.

**Grounded.** Claims tie to numbers, timeframes, or named examples. "Save 5 to 10 hours a week" beats "unlock transformative productivity."

**Generous.** Give value before asking for anything. The free briefing, the readiness assessment, and the prompt library are the voice made operational.

**Direct.** Say the hard thing kindly. "AI will not fix a broken process. It will make a broken process faster." That sentence sells more than a benefit list.

**Warm, not chummy.** Address the reader as *you*. Speak as *we*, or as *I* when Josh is personally the deliverer. Never "utilize," never "synergy," never exclamation points stacked for enthusiasm.

## We say / we do not say

| We say | We do not say |
|---|---|
| Tools you can use Monday | Cutting-edge, next-generation, revolutionary |
| Save 5 to 10 hours a week | Unlock exponential productivity |
| Here is what it costs | Contact us for a custom quote (when a price exists) |
| This will not work for your situation | Every business needs AI |
| We will show you how | We will handle it for you |
| Northern Colorado businesses | Businesses nationwide |
| A working process in 90 days | Digital transformation journey |
| Practical AI | AI-powered, AI-driven, AI-first (as filler) |

## Words to retire

Leverage (as a verb), robust, seamless, game-changer, disrupt, unlock, empower, journey, solutioning, best-in-class, paradigm, "in today's fast-paced world."

## Words that carry the brand

Practical. Working. Measurable. Local. Hours saved. Monday. Weeks, not years. Show you. Right-sized. Pays for itself.

## Grammar and mechanics

- No em dashes. Use a period, a comma, or a colon.
- Oxford comma: yes.
- Numerals for anything countable: 5 to 10 hours, 3 workshops, 90 days.
- Spell dollar amounts as $129, $2,200, $11,250. No decimals on whole dollars.
- Second person by default. "Your team" not "the client's team."
- Contractions are fine and preferred. They make the writing sound like a person.
- One idea per sentence. One idea per bullet.

## Tone by channel

| Channel | Tone | Length |
|---|---|---|
| Flyer / print | Direct, benefit-forward, scannable | Headline + 4 bullets + one CTA |
| LinkedIn article | Teaching voice. One lesson, one example, one takeaway | 400 to 700 words |
| Email to a warm lead | Personal, specific to their business, one clear ask | Under 150 words |
| Proposal / SOW | Structured, plain, outcome-anchored. Headings and rationale | As long as it needs, no longer |
| In the room | Conversational, question-led, generous with demos | Talk less than half the time |

## How faith shows up

The business plan names integrity, honesty, and stewardship as founding values. That belongs in how the brand behaves, not in what the brand says. Idea Collaborative does not market as a faith-based business. It earns the reputation through transparent pricing, honest scoping, refusing work that will not help, and following through after the invoice clears. A client should be able to tell something is different about how they were treated without being told why.

Through hands-on workshops and practical implementation, we help owners and operators turn AI from a headline into a working process, with 25+ years of enterprise solutions experience behind every recommendation.

He founded Idea Collaborative to bring that same discipline to the businesses in his own community. - Based in Loveland, serving Fort Collins, Windsor, and the Front Range

# 9. Copy Patterns

## Headline formulas

- **Timeframe:** "Transform your business with AI in weeks, not years."
- **Cost of inaction:** "Your competitors started six months ago."
- **Specific relief:** "Get your Saturdays back."
- **Plain question:** "What would you do with 8 extra hours a week?"

Avoid formulas built on fear or shame. Skepticism converts to trust; anxiety converts to nothing.

## Benefit bullet pattern

Verb, specific outcome, timeframe or scale.

> ✓ Save 5 to 10 hours per week immediately
> ✓ Automate repetitive business processes
> ✓ Get enterprise AI expertise at SMB prices
> ✓ Local expert serving Loveland, Fort Collins, and Windsor

Four bullets is the ceiling on any single surface. Lead with the strongest.

## Cold email pattern

1. One line naming something specific and true about their business
2. One line on a problem businesses like theirs are solving with AI
3. One sentence on what Idea Collaborative does, in plain terms
4. One low-friction ask (15 minutes, or a seat at the next briefing)
5. Sign-off with name and phone

Under 150 words. No attachments on first contact.

## LinkedIn post pattern

Open with a real scenario. Show the before and after with a number. Name the tool. Close with one takeaway the reader can act on without hiring anyone. No engagement bait, no "Agree?", no comment-farming.

# 10. Two Things to Fix

Flagging these because the brand's stated value is honesty and the current flyer undercuts it slightly.

**1. The social proof claim.** The flyer reads "Join Northern Colorado businesses already using AI to save time and grow revenue." That is defensible as a statement about the market, but a reader will hear it as a claim about your client base. Until there are named clients, use a market-true version: "Northern Colorado businesses are already using AI to save time and grow revenue. Here is how to start." Once there are three case studies, replace it with a real one.

**2. Pricing asymmetry.** Showing $129 next to two cards reading "Call for More Info" invites the assumption that Levels 2 and 3 are expensive enough to hide. Either list all three prices or list none. Listing all three is stronger; the plan already sets them, and transparency is a differentiator against national training vendors.
